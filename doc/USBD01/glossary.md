# Glossary

| **_TEA_** (EN)                  | **_TEA_** (PT)                         | **_Description_** (EN)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |                                       
|:--------------------------------|:---------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Acceptance Criteria**         | **Critérios de Aceitação**             | Conditions that a software product must satisfy to be accepted by the user or system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Acceleration (Locomotive)**   | **Aceleração (Locomotiva)**            | A locomotive's rate of speed increase, affecting train performance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Aisle**                       | **Corredor**                           | Open space between rows of bays in a warehouse that provides pathways to move goods and access storage areas.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Audit Log**                   | **Registo de Auditoria**               | File that records all inspection and processing operations with relevant details for accountability and traceability purposes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Backlog**                     | **Backlog**                            | A list of task or features to be completed in a project.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Bay**                         | **Baía**                               | Specific storage area within an aisle that can store a limited number of product boxes, identified by warehouse, aisle and bay coordinates.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Best Fit Decreasing**         | **Melhor Ajuste Descendente**          | Packing heuristic that sorts allocations by weight from largest to smallest and places each item into the trolley that will result in the smallest amount of remaining unused capacity, achieving the tightest fit.                                                                                                                           |
| **BFD**                         | **MAD**                                | Acronym for Best Fit Decreasing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Box**                         | **Caixa**                              | Small lot fragment of one Stock Keeping Unit with quantity and optional expiry date, transported in train wagons and stored in warehouse bays.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Boxcar**                      | **Vagão de Carga**                     | Enclosed freight car designed to transport general goods.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **C/Assembly**                  | **C/Assembly**                         | Low-level programming languages used for system programming and performance-critical applications.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **CamelCase**                   | **CamelCase**                          | A naming convention in programming where words are concatenated without spaces, capitalizing each word.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Cargo**                       | **Mercadoria**                         | Goods or materials transported by trains, such as coal, passengers, or mail.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Chemical Tank Car**           | **Vagão Cisterna Químico**             | Specialized tank car for transporting chemicals with varying densities and high purity specifications, such as styrene and jet fuel.                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Circulation Lines**           | **Linhas de Circulação**               | Tracks where wagons circulate to reach the parking areas or unloading docks.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Comma-Separated Values**      | **Valores Separados por Vírgulas**     | A simple file format used to store tabular data, where each line represents a data record and each field is separated by a comma.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Conceptual Data Model**       | **Modelo de Dados Conceptual**         | High-level abstraction of the data in the railway domain, describing the main entities and their relationships without implementation details.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **CSV**                         | **CSV**                                | Acronym for Comma-Separated Values.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Customer Remorse**            | **Remorso do Cliente**                 | Return reason where the customer returned the item in good condition due to change of mind, unwanted gift, or wrong choice. These items can often be restocked after inspection.                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Cycle Count**                 | **Contagem Cíclica**                   | Return reason where a mismatch was found during a stock audit. These items are not faulty but must be rechecked before restocking.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Damaged**                     | **Danificado**                         | Return reason indicating the product is physically broken or unusable. These items must be discarded and cannot be restocked.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Database**                    | **Base de Dados**                      | Central repository that stores and manages all information related to the railway system, including trains, stations, tracks, and operations. It ensures data integrity and supports the execution of user stories defined for the system.                                                                                                    |
| **Depot**                       | **Depósito**                           | Type of building to storage cargo, trains will load/unload there.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Dock**                        | **Doca**                               | Access point to warehouses where goods are unloaded from railway wagons for storage or processing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Editor**                      | **Editor**                             | A user responsible for creating and editing maps and scenarios, including the placement of cities and industries, and the definition of scenario restrictions.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Electrified Rail Tracks**     | **Trilho Eletrificado**                | A track that supports electric locomotives.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Expired**                     | **Expirado**                           | Return reason indicating the product has passed its expiry date. These items cannot be sold and must be discarded.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **FEFO**                        | **PEPS**                               | Acronym for First-Expired-First-Out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **FF**                          | **PE**                                 | Acronym for First Fit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **FFD**                         | **PED**                                | Acronym for First Fit Decreasing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **FIFO**                        | **PEPS**                               | Acronym for First-In-First-Out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **First Fit**                   | **Primeiro Encaixe**                   | Packing heuristic that places each item in the first available trolley where it fits, following the input order of the allocation rows.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **First Fit Decreasing**        | **Primeiro Encaixe Descendente**       | Packing heuristic that sorts unpicked allocations by weight from largest to smallest and places each item in the first available trolley where it fits.                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **First-Expired-First-Out**     | **Primeiro-a-Expirar-Primeiro-a-Sair** | Inventory management principle for perishable goods where products with earliest expiry dates are dispatched first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **First-In-First-Out**          | **Primeiro-a-Entrar-Primeiro-a-Sair**  | Inventory management principle for non-perishable goods where products received first are dispatched first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Flatcar**                     | **Vagão de Plataforma**                | Open, flat-surfaced freight car used for transporting containers, vehicles or machinery.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Freight**                     | **Mercadorias**                        | Goods or materials transported by trains, typically using specialized freight cars (boxcars, flatcars, tank cars, hopper cars, etc.) as part of rail logistics operations.                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Gauge**                       | **Bitola**                             | Distance between the inner sides of the two parallel rails that make up a railway track.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Grain Silo**                  | **Silo de Grãos**                      | A building present on stations or terminals that stores grains.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Hopper Car**                  | **Vagão-Tremonha**                     | Freight car used for transporting bulk materials such as coal, grains, or minerals.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Industrial Hubs**             | **Centros Industriais**                | Location with a high density of industries.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Intermodal Transport**        | **Transporte Intermodal**              | The use of multiple modes of transportation (Rail combined with trucks/ships) for "door-to-door" logistics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Java**                        | **Java**                               | A high-level programming language used for developing the application.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **JavaDoc**                     | **JavaDoc**                            | A documentation generator for Java code, producing HTML documentation from comments in the source code.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Line**                        | **Linha**                              | Physical routes on which freight trains travel, built to connect stations and terminals, often connect industrial zones, ports, cities, and distribution centers.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Loading/Unloading**           | **Carregamento/Descarregamento**       | The process of placing cargo onto or removing cargo from trains at terminals,depots,ports, or industrial hubs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Locomotive**                  | **Locomotiva**                         | Engines that pull the freight trains. Locomotives can be powered by electricity or diesel fuel.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Logbook**                     | **Livro de Registo**                   | Document that is used to track the progress of a project that is performed over multiple days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Mineral Oil Car**             | **Vagão de Óleo Mineral**              | Tank car designed for the transport of various petrochemicals and mineral oils.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Minimum Viable Product**      | **Produto Mínimo Viável**              | First functional version of the system that demonstrates the essential features of a functionality.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **MVP**                         | **MPV**                                | Acronym for _Minimal Viable Product_.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Object-Oriented Programming** | **Programação Orientada a Objetos**    | Programming paradigm based on the concept of objects. Objects can contain data and have actions they can perform.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **OOP**                         | **POO**                                | Acronym for Object-Oriented Programming.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Oracle LiveSQL**              | **Oracle LiveSQL**                     | Online platform provided by Oracle to execute and test SQL scripts in a shared database environment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Parking Lines**               | **Linhas de Estacionamento**           | Sidings where wagons wait temporarily before being sent to another destination.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Path**                        | **Caminho**                            | Ordered list of stations through which the train must pass (not necessarily stopping) to travel from station A to station B.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Physical Data Model**         | **Modelo Físico de Dados**             | Implementation-level representation of the data model, including database tables, constraints, and indexes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Picker**                      | **Recolhedor**                         | Warehouse worker responsible for collecting products from specific locations in the warehouse to fulfill orders.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **PL/SQL**                      | **PL/SQL**                             | A procedural extension of SQL used in Oracle databases to write complex queries and manage database operations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Pressure Gas Car**            | **Vagão a Gás sob Pressão**            | Tank car used to transport liquefied gases such as propane, butane, and other gaseous mixtures under pressure.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Product Owner**               | **Dono do Produto**                    | A user responsible for defining the product vision  and ensuring that the development team delivers value to the business.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Production Scenarios**        | **Cenários de Produção**               | -------------------------                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Refrigerated Car**            | **Vagão Frigorífico**                  | Freight car equipped with cooling systems for transporting perishable goods.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Relational Model**            | **Modelo Relacional**                  | Logical representation of the railway system's data organized in interconnected tables, defining relationships between entities such as stations, locomotives, and line segments.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Route**                       | **Rota**                               | A predefined path (sequence of stations) assigned to a single freight or multiple freights.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Scalable Vector Graphics**    | **Gráficos Vetoriais Escaláveis**      | A file format for vector graphics, allowing for scalable images without loss of quality.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Schedulers/Planners**         | **Planeadores**                        | A user responsible for analysing, designing and adjusting train timetables to maximize efficiency.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Scheduling**                  | **Planeamento**                        | The process of planning train timetables to ensure efficient and timely operations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **SCRUM**                       | **SCRUM**                              | An agile framework for managing complex projects, emphasizing iterative progress, collaboration, and adaptability.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Scrum Board**                 | **Quadro Scrum**                       | A visual tool used in Scrum to track the progress of tasks during a sprint, typically divided into columns such as "To Do," "In Progress," and "Done."                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Scrum Master**                | **Scrum Master**                       | A facilitator for an agile development team, ensuring that the Scrum framework is followed and helping to remove obstacles.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **SKU**                         | **UME**                                | Acronym for Stock Keeping Unit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **SQL**                         | **SQL**                                | Acronym for Structured Query Language.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **SQL DDL**                     | **SQL DDL**                            | Subset of SQL commands used to define and modify database structures such as tables and constraints.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Sprint**                      | **Sprint**                             | A time-boxed period in Scrum during which specific work has to be completed and made ready for review.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Station**                     | **Estação**                            | A facility serving cities/industries with the purpose of loading, unloading, sorting, and assembling trains.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Station Master**              | **Chefe de Estação**                   | A user responsible for overseeing station operations, coordinate arrivals and departures.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Stock Keeping Unit**          | **Unidade de Manutenção de Estoque**   | Unique identifier for each distinct product type, used for inventory control and management with associated attributes such as name, category, volume, and weight.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Structured Query Language**   | **Linguagem de Consulta Estruturada**  | A standardized programming language used to manage and manipulate relational databases.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Supply Chain**                | **Cadeia de Mercadoria**               | Entire network of organizations, people, activities, information, and resources involved in creating and delivering a product or service from its origin to the end customer.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **SVG**                         | **SVG**                                | Acronym for Scalable Vector Graphics                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Tank Car**                    | **Vagão Cisterna**                     | Freight car used for transporting liquids, chemicals, or fuels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **TDD**                         | **TDD**                                | Acronym for Test-Driven Development.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Terminal**                    | **Terminal**                           | A large station type, serving as a central hub for cargo. It may be intermodal, where containers can be transferred between trains.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Test-Driven Development**     | **Desenvolvimento Guiado por Testes**  | A development process where tests are written before implementing code.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Track**                       | **Trilho**                             | Structure of steel rails on which trains run.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Train**                       | **Comboio**                            | A connected series of rail vehicles (locomotive and wagons/freight cars) operating on the network.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Train Driver**                | **Condutor de Comboio**                | A user responsible for operating trains, follow schedules and ensuring safe movement.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Trolley**                     | **Carrinho**                           | Equipment used by pickers to collect and transport items during picking operations, with a defined weight capacity that must be respected.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Unloading Docks**             | **Docas de Descarregamento**           | Access points to warehouses, where goods are unloaded for longer storage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **User Story**                  | **História de Utilizador**             | A brief description of a feature from the perspective of an user.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Visual Paradigm**             | **Visual Paradigm**                    | Software tool used to design conceptual, logical, and physical data models and automatically generate SQL scripts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Wagons/Freight Cars**         | **Vagões/Vagões de Carga**             | Railway vehicle designed to transport specific types of goods, such as containers, liquids, fuels, or bulk materials. Common types include boxcars, flatcars, tank cars, hopper cars, and refrigerated cars. Relevant characteristics include payload, volume capacity, dimensions, and tare weight.                                          |
| **Warehouse**                   | **Armazém**                            | Storage facility connected to docks, organized in aisles and bays, where goods unloaded from wagons are stored for longer periods.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Warehouse Management System** | **Sistema de Gestão de Armazém**       | System that meticulously records all movements of goods including unloading, relocation, transfers, and dispatch, ensuring accurate stock tracking by SKU and storage location with full traceability.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Warehouse Planner**           | **Planeador de Armazém**               | User responsible for fulfilling orders by verifying inventory availability, allocating quantities from boxes, and generating per-line statuses and allocation rows for dispatch planning.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Warehousing**                 | **Armazenamento**                      | The process of storing cargo at freight yards, terminals and sidings.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **WMS**                         | **SGA**                                | Acronym for Warehouse Management System.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
